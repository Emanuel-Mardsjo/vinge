Photography library

Twenty-nine supplied photographs of the offices and the people in them. They are the only real photography the system holds; everything else is still a labelled placeholder. Natural light, no filters, no grain, no duotone — they already sit on the paper/ink axis, so nothing is graded on the way in.

Every image is cropped by Figure to one of the three ratios (3:2, 4:5, 21:9) with object-fit: cover, so a set's own aspect matters less than where the subject sits in frame. The four sets are named for what the photograph does, not for what is in it. Files are in assets/images/.

anonymous — 9 files

Rooms with people in them, nobody identifiable. The workhorse set: scene setting at 3:2 and 21:9, and the only set that survives a wide crop.

inviting — 6 files

Two people meeting, arriving, walking together. Use where the page is addressing the reader — a CTA block, a contact route, the top of a people section.

present — 6 files

One or two people at work, held at a distance that reads as a person rather than a portrait. Closest thing the library has to a stand-in for a named individual, and the set to use at 4:5 until portraits are shot.

office — 8 files

Rooms, surfaces and objects, with a figure passing at most. The quiet set: use it where an image is a pause between two text blocks, never where the image has to carry the section.

Rules

No image is graded, tinted or duotoned. Text over one of these needs a solid ink panel behind it, as everywhere else. The library holds no individual portraits and no photograph of a client's own world — both are still to be commissioned, and until then a block that needs them borrows the nearest set and says so in its caption.
