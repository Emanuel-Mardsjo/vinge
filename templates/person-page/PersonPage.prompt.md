# Usage Notes

Source: DevOps #95745

## Editorial Requirements

- Editors must be able to manage person information.

## Expected Content / Sections

The page presents an individual person and may contain:

- Name
- Practice areas / sectors
- Portrait
- Identity information
- Contact information
- Biography
- Cases
- Recognitions
- Background
- Published content
- Testimonials
- Related experts

## Open Questions

- Which sections are required and which are optional?
- Which sections are fixed, and which can be added, removed, or reordered by editors?
- Is person information managed directly on the page or retrieved from a shared people directory?
