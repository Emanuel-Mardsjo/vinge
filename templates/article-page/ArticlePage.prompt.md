# Usage Notes

Source: DevOps #95723

## Editorial Requirements

- Editors must be able to manage the article's editorial content.

## Expected Content / Sections

The article must contain a main heading and main content.

The article may additionally contain:

- Featured image
- Article metadata
- Author information
- Table of contents
- Further reading

## Open Questions

- Which content sections are required and which are optional?
- Which content sections are edited directly, and which may be managed through other sources or mechanisms?
